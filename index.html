How a project runs

Every engagement follows the same five steps, with a fixed quote agreed after the audit.

How we work

  1. Audit

    We review your devices, accounts and current settings, then send a written report.

  2. Design

    We agree the security baseline, apps and enrollment flow for each platform.

  3. Pilot

    A small test group uses the new setup for a week while we fix rough edges.

  4. Roll out

    Teams move over in planned batches, with a guide for staff.

  5. Run

    We hand over full documentation, or keep running it on a monthly plan.

Common questions

What is unified endpoint management?

Looking after every laptop, desktop and phone your company owns from one place: setting devices up, keeping them updated and secure, and locking or erasing them if they are lost. UEM in our name stands for it.

How much does it cost?

Projects are quoted at a fixed price after a free device audit. Ongoing support is priced per device, per month.

Do you only work in Ghaziabad?

No. Most of our work is done remotely, so we can help companies anywhere. We visit offices when a project needs it.

Do we need to buy new software?

Often not. Many companies already pay for Intune through Microsoft 365, or own a tool they barely use. We start with what you have.
