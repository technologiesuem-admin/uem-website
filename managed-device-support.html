Why a good setup drifts

Device management is not a one-time project. People join and leave, apps update every week, and Apple and Microsoft change how things work several times a year.

Without someone looking after it, a well-built setup falls out of date within months.

What's included

  • Joiners and leaversNew devices ready for day one; leavers' devices locked, wiped and reassigned.
  • Patch managementOperating system and app updates tracked, with late devices followed up.
  • App packagingNew and updated apps tested and published to your app catalogue.
  • Policy changesChanges you ask for, tested on a pilot group before they go live.
  • Monthly health reportDevice count, compliance, encryption, update status and anything that needs a decision.
  • Quarterly reviewA call about what changed, what Apple and Microsoft have coming, and what to improve.
  • Help for your IT teamSecond-line support when your own team gets stuck, with a response within one day.

How it runs

  1. Onboard

    Audit your current setup and access.

  2. Fix

    Resolve anything urgent first.

  3. Run

    Monthly patching, changes and reports.

  4. Review

    A quarterly call to plan ahead.

What you get at the end

Everything is in your company's name and documented, so you are never tied to us.

  • A monthly health report for your devices
  • Joiner and leaver handling to an agreed checklist
  • A quarterly review call
  • Up-to-date documentation of your setup

Questions

Do we need an IT team of our own?

No. We can be your device team, or work alongside your IT staff and handle the specialist parts.

Can we start with support if someone else built our setup?

Yes. We start with an audit, fix anything urgent, then take over the monthly work.

Is there a minimum contract?

We agree the term with you when we quote, based on the number of devices and the support you need.
