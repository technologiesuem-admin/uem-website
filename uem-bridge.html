A set of tools for moving Macs between device management platforms, such as Jamf Pro to Microsoft Intune. Check every Mac before you start, keep Macs connected during the switch, and guide each person through their move.

Why Mac migrations are slow

Moving a Mac to a new MDM means removing the old management, enrolling in the new one, and keeping Wi-Fi, VPN, sign-in and encryption working in between.

Done by hand, every Mac needs an IT visit or a long list of instructions, and the gap between the two platforms is where things break.

Seven tools, one migration

Each part of UEM Bridge for Mac handles one stage of the move.

Before the move

UEM Bridge Scan

A read-only readiness check on every Mac, plus a fleet report showing what will break, who is blocked and why.

UEM Bridge Translate

Reads your current MDM's profiles and policies and produces the new MDM's equivalents, or a gap report where there isn't one.

During the move

UEM Bridge Console

The central admin tool. Moves waves of Macs in Apple Business Manager, holds the old MDM's API keys, and gives you a dashboard and audit export.

UEM Bridge Move

The Mac app and background worker that run the switch on each Mac and show the user every step.

UEM Bridge Keep

Keeps each Mac connected. It snapshots certificates, restores them after the old MDM leaves, and removes them once the new MDM's certificates arrive.

UEM Bridge Identity

Handles accounts during the move: an account mapping file, Jamf Connect to Platform SSO, rescuing admin passwords and rotating FileVault keys.

After the move

UEM Bridge Ops

One inventory, drift detection and audit evidence across several MDMs.

Coming later

How a rollout works

  1. Scan

    Run UEM Bridge Scan and fix what the fleet report flags.

  2. Translate

    Turn your current policies into the new MDM's settings.

  3. Pilot

    Move a small wave of Macs from the Console and check each one.

  4. Move in waves

    Each person follows the on-screen steps while Keep holds their connections.

  5. Close

    Confirm every Mac reports to the new MDM, then retire the old one.
