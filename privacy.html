Privacy notice

How UEM Technologies Private Limited handles the personal data you share through this website.

Who we are

UEM Technologies Private Limited, Shop-01, MM-52, Ground Floor, DLF Ankur Vihar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. Contact: sales@uemtechnologies.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect your name, company, work email, phone number if you give it, the number and type of devices you have, and your message.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to provide our services. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

  • Our email routing and inbox providers
  • Our email provider
  • Our website host, which keeps short-lived technical logs

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for one month. If you do not become a client, we delete them after that.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing sales@uemtechnologies.com.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Demo videos are hosted on YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, and the player loads only when you press play. Video preview images are loaded from YouTube.
