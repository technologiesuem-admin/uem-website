Platforms we work with

We recommend the tool that fits your company, including the ones you already pay for. Here is what each platform is and how we use it.

Jamf Pro

What it isDevice management built for Apple: Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

What we do with itFull setups, migrations to and from Jamf, security baselines and Self Service catalogues.

Addigy

What it isCloud-based Apple device management with a simpler console.

What we do with itSetups for smaller Apple fleets and for IT providers who manage several companies.

Microsoft Intune

What it isMicrosoft's cloud tool for managing Windows, Android, iPhone and Mac.

What we do with itAutopilot, compliance, Conditional Access, app deployment and Android Enterprise.

Apple Business Manager

What it isApple's free portal for company-owned devices, apps and work accounts.

What we do with itAccount setup, reseller links and automatic enrollment into your management tool.

Windows Autopilot

What it isMicrosoft's way of setting up new PCs straight from the box.

What we do with itEnrollment profiles, device registration and remote reset of existing PCs.

Android Enterprise

What it isGoogle's framework for work profiles and company-owned Android devices.

What we do with itWork profiles, fully managed phones and single-app kiosk devices through Intune.

Microsoft Entra ID

What it isMicrosoft's identity service, formerly called Azure Active Directory.

What we do with itDevice join, single sign-on, Conditional Access and Mac sign-in with work accounts.

Delinea

What it isPrivileged access tools, including Secret Server and Privilege Manager.

What we do with itAdmin rights removal, time-limited elevation and vaulting of shared admin passwords.
