Careers at UEM Technologies

Join a small team that sets up and runs device management for growing companies.

What the work is like

You work on real client setups from your first month: building Jamf, Intune and Addigy environments, moving companies between platforms, and writing documentation people actually read.

Skills we look for

Nobody has all of these on day one.

  • Device management platformsHands-on experience with Jamf Pro, Microsoft Intune or Addigy.
  • macOS and WindowsComfortable administering both, from the command line as well as the console.
  • ScriptingBash, zsh or PowerShell for the jobs a console cannot do.
  • IdentityMicrosoft Entra ID, Google Workspace and Apple Business Manager.
  • Clear writingDocumentation and client emails that people understand the first time.
  • CuriosityApple and Microsoft change things every year. You enjoy keeping up.

Open roles

There are no open roles right now. We still like hearing from people with device management experience, so send us your CV and we will get in touch when a role opens.

How to apply

Email your CV to sales@uemtechnologies.com with "Careers" in the subject line, plus a few lines about a device management problem you solved.
