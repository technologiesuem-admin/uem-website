The questions customers now ask

Customer security questionnaires ask exactly how you protect laptops. Are disks encrypted? Do updates install within a set time? Can a lost device be wiped?

Many companies already own the tools to say yes, but not the settings or the evidence.

What's included

  • Gap assessmentYour current device settings compared with CIS Benchmarks, with each gap ranked by risk.
  • Encryption everywhereFileVault and BitLocker enforced, with recovery keys stored centrally.
  • Patch deadlinesOperating system and browser updates installed within the window you choose.
  • Screen lock and firewallBasic controls enforced on every device, not left to each user.
  • Endpoint protectionMicrosoft Defender for Endpoint, CrowdStrike, SentinelOne or your chosen tool, deployed through your management platform.
  • Compliance reportingDashboards that show which devices meet policy and which need attention.
  • Audit evidenceExports, screenshots and written procedures mapped to ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls for endpoint devices.
  • Questionnaire answersAccurate answers for the device sections of customer security questionnaires.

How it runs

  1. Assess

    Compare current settings with CIS Benchmarks.

  2. Prioritise

    Rank gaps by risk and effort.

  3. Apply

    Roll out baselines, pilot first.

  4. Report

    Set up compliance dashboards.

  5. Review

    Check results every quarter.

What you get at the end

Everything is in your company's name and documented, so you are never tied to us.

  • A gap report ranked by risk
  • Security baselines applied to every managed device
  • Compliance dashboards your team can check any time
  • An evidence pack mapped to ISO 27001 and SOC 2 endpoint controls

Questions

Can you get us ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certified?

We prepare the device controls and the evidence. Certification itself is issued by an accredited auditor after their own review.

Will tighter security slow our team down?

We pilot every change with a small group first and adjust anything that gets in the way of real work.

Do we need new security software?

Often not. Many companies already own the tools through Microsoft 365 or their device management platform. We start with what you have.
