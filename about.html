About UEM Technologies

An IT company in Ghaziabad that does one thing well: managing and securing the devices a business runs on.

What UEM means

UEM stands for unified endpoint management. It means looking after every laptop, desktop and phone a company owns from one place: setting devices up, keeping them updated and secure, and locking or erasing them when they go missing.

UEM Technologies Private Limited does this work for companies that don't have a device specialist of their own. Our team works hands-on with Jamf Pro, Microsoft Intune, Addigy and privileged access tools.

Built on 3 years of hands-on endpoint engineering and formally incorporated in June 2024, UEM Technologies was started to give growing companies enterprise-grade device management and zero-data-loss migrations without wiping employee laptops or slowing teams down.

How we work

Five principles that apply to every project, large or small.

  • Advice that fits youWe recommend the platform that suits your devices and budget, including tools you already pay for.
  • A fixed quote before work startsYou know the cost after the audit, not after the invoice.
  • Pilot before rolloutEvery change is tested with a small group before it reaches everyone.
  • Documentation you keepEvery setting we change is written down for your team.
  • Your accounts stay yoursAdmin access and licences are always in your company's name.

Our process

  1. Audit

    We review your devices, accounts and current settings, then send a written report.

  2. Design

    We agree the security baseline, apps and enrollment flow for each platform.

  3. Pilot

    A small test group uses the new setup for a week while we fix rough edges.

  4. Roll out

    Teams move over in planned batches, with a guide for staff.

  5. Run

    We hand over full documentation, or keep running it on a monthly plan.
